Epic and clinical applications

Workflow analysis and application support from the analyst's chair: where orders stall, what the help desk sees after an upgrade, and what to fix first.

Medication Order Workflow Analysis

Where does order-to-dispense fall behind, and which workflow exceptions cause it? Built around a verify, dispense prep, and dispense tracking flow, using 1,500 synthetic orders.

SQL (PostgreSQL), KPI design, root-cause analysis

Read the case studyDataSQL
  • 85.3% of orders on time overall
  • Exceptions drop on-time from 88.8% to 52.7%
  • Oncology verification takes a median 23.8 min, vs 13 to 16 elsewhere

Clinical Application Support Ticket Analysis

Where are support SLAs missed, what drives ticket volume, and what happened after an upgrade? 900 synthetic tickets across six months.

SQL (PostgreSQL), SLA analysis, root-cause analysis

Read the case studyDataSQL
  • P1 tickets meet SLA only 64.9% of the time
  • Upgrade week: 71 tickets vs 31.9 a normal week
  • 26.8% of tickets are knowledge gaps, only 22% had an article

Revenue cycle

Two ends of the revenue cycle: making sure what was dispensed gets billed correctly, and working denials and A/R where the dollars are.

Pharmacy Charge Capture and Revenue Integrity Audit

Do billed charges match what pharmacy dispensed, and where is revenue lost or overbilled? Reconciles 4,000 synthetic dispense records against charges.

SQL (PostgreSQL), reconciliation, charge master review

Read the case studyDataSQL
  • Charge capture of 93.7%, a $47,295 net gap
  • 51.8% of updated biologics billed at a stale price
  • 48 of 183 returned items were still billed, a compliance risk

Claims Denial and A/R Analysis

Which denials are preventable, which appeals are worth working, and where is A/R aging? 3,000 synthetic claims across six generic payers.

SQL (PostgreSQL), denial root-cause, appeal prioritization, A/R aging

Read the case studyDataSQL
  • 45.2% of denied dollars start at the front end
  • Up to about $71K recoverable from unappealed high-win denials
  • Commercial C averages 88.1 days in A/R vs about 50 for the best payers
