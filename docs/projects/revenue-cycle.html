Business question

How can a healthcare organization quickly identify where claims performance and reimbursement risk require operational attention?

Project evidence

  • Interactive filters for payer, location, service line, and claim status.
  • KPIs for charges, allowed amount, paid amount, collection rate, denial rate, A/R, and denied dollars.

Case study narrative

What the analysis demonstrates

Methodology

  1. Defined operational and financial KPIs before analysis.
  2. Validated required fields, status values, date ranges, and numeric columns.
  3. Segmented performance by the business dimensions most likely to guide action.
  4. Separated volume, rate, financial impact, and aging so priorities were not based on counts alone.
  5. Translated findings into a measurable operational recommendation.

Key findings

  • Overall collection rate is 65.6% against allowed amount.
  • 349 claims were denied, representing $531,071 in denied allowed amount.
  • Average days in A/R is 57.4, making aging and denial prevention joint priorities.
  • Commercial A and Medicare show the highest denial rates in the full sample, while payer collection performance varies materially.

Recommendations

  • Establish a weekly payer scorecard with denial rate, collection rate, A/R, and denied dollars.
  • Route high-dollar denials to prioritized workqueues rather than using claim count alone.
  • Create pre-bill edits for recurring authorization, eligibility, documentation, and coding defects.
  • Assign owners and 30/60/90-day targets for denial prevention and aging reduction.

Limitations and next steps

  • The data is synthetic and does not include patient, provider, contract, staffing, or clinical-acuity details.
  • Estimated financial impact uses allowed or simulated savings amounts; it is not a guaranteed recovery value.
  • A production implementation should add data-quality checks, security controls, scheduled refreshes, ownership, targets, and validated business definitions.
  • The next build would connect the same logic to governed source systems and a production BI semantic model.
