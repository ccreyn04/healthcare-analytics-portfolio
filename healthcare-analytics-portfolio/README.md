# Healthcare Analytics Portfolio

Four end-to-end healthcare analytics case studies by **Cierra Reynolds, CPhT,
CRCR, CSPR**.

This portfolio demonstrates how I translate healthcare operations and
reimbursement questions into validated KPIs, auditable analysis, executive
dashboards, and practical recommendations.

## Portfolio projects

| Project | Business focus | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Revenue Cycle Executive Dashboard](projects/01-revenue-cycle/) | Cash capture, payer variation, denials, and A/R risk | 2,000 synthetic claims, SQL, CSV, executive dashboard |
| [Claims Denial Root-Cause Analysis](projects/02-claims-denials/) | Denial rate, denied value, causes, payer exposure, and aging | 349 denied claims, denominator-aware SQL, dashboard |
| [Pharmacy Operations Dashboard](projects/03-pharmacy-operations/) | Medication turnaround, on-time reliability, locations, and exceptions | 1,200 synthetic medication orders, SQL, dashboard |
| [Prior Authorization Access Dashboard](projects/04-prior-authorization/) | Approval performance, backlog, aging, payer variation, and value protected | 700 synthetic cases, SQL, dashboard |

## Analytical approach

1. **Define** the business decision, data grain, denominator, and KPI logic.
2. **Validate** record counts, dates, categories, numeric fields, and
   reconciliation totals.
3. **Analyze** volume, rate, value, variation, and aging.
4. **Recommend** specific actions, owners, and measurable follow-through.

## Data ethics

All datasets are synthetic and PHI-free. The findings are calculated from the
downloadable source data and demonstrate analytical method. They do not
represent a real patient, payer, employer, or guaranteed financial result.
